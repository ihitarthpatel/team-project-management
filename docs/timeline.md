# 📅 Timeline

## Project Schedule

| Week   | Phase                      | Key Activities                              |
|--------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Week 1 | Planning & Task Assignment | Define goals, assign roles, create strategy |
| Week 2 | Research & Planning        | Shortlist venues, contact speakers, risk plan |
| Week 3 | Design & Development       | Design materials, build registration, promote |
| Week 4 | Final Preparation          | Testing, dry run, event execution            |
| Week 5 | Post-Event Review          | Feedback collection, analysis, final report  |

---

### Week 1 — Planning & Task Assignment
- Formed the team and assigned roles
- Defined event objectives and theme
- Created project timeline and budget
- Set up communication channels

### Week 2 — Research & Planning
- Shortlisted 3 potential venues
- Identified and contacted 4 speakers
- Created risk management plan
- Conducted weekly meeting

### Week 3 — Design & Development
- Event poster designed and approved
- Registration form built and deployed
- Venue booked and speakers confirmed
- Social media promotion launched

### Week 4 — Final Preparation
- Registration system tested — 67 registrations received
- Certificates designed and printed
- Refreshments arranged and event day checklist finalized
- Dry run completed successfully

### Week 5 — Post-Event Review
- Event executed with 58 attendees (of 67 registered)
- 88% participant satisfaction rate achieved
- Final report and retrospective completed
- Key learnings documented for future events
