## 7. Lessons Learned

1. **Early planning pays off** — Starting 5 weeks ahead prevented last-minute issues
2. **Clear role definition** — Each member knowing their tasks reduced confusion
3. **Regular communication** — Weekly meetings kept the team aligned
4. **Contingency planning** — Having backup plans ensured smooth execution
5. **Feedback is essential** — Post-event feedback provided valuable insights
