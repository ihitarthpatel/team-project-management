## 🏗 Detailed Role Breakdown

### 🔷 Team Leader — Hitarth Dhaduk
- Define project scope and objectives
- Create and maintain project timeline
- Assign and monitor tasks across the team
- Conduct weekly team meetings
- Resolve conflicts and remove blockers
- Final decision-making authority

### 🎨 Designer — Member 2
- Design event posters and social media graphics
- Create presentation templates for workshops
- Design certificates for participants
- Maintain visual consistency across all materials
- Coordinate with Team Leader for design approvals

### 💻 Developer — Member 3
- Build and deploy the online registration form
- Set up event webpage/landing page
- Manage participant database
- Provide technical support during the event
- Test all systems before event day

### 📋 Coordinator — Member 4
- Handle venue booking and logistics
- Manage communication with speakers/guests
- Coordinate refreshments and supplies
- Oversee volunteer management on event day
- Distribute certificates and collect feedback

## 🤝 Decision-Making Process

1. **Minor Decisions** → Individual role owners can decide independently
2. **Moderate Decisions** → Discuss in team meeting, majority consensus
3. **Major Decisions** → Team Leader makes final call after team input

---

> *Each team member is accountable for their deliverables and is expected to report progress weekly.*
