# ⚠️ Risk Management

## Risks & Solutions

| Risk                  | Impact | Solution                              |
|-----------------------|--------|---------------------------------------|
| Delay in tasks        | High   | Daily follow-ups and progress checks  |
| Miscommunication      | High   | Clear role assignment and channels    |
| Low participation     | Medium | Early promotion across multiple platforms |
| Technical failures    | Medium | Test all systems one day before event |
| Budget overrun        | Low    | Track expenses weekly with 10% buffer |

---

### Risk Response Actions

**Risk: Delay in Tasks**
- Conducted daily follow-ups via WhatsApp
- Set clear deadlines with accountability
- Team Leader intervened early on blockers

**Risk: Miscommunication**
- Defined clear roles and responsibilities from Day 1
- Used dedicated communication channels
- Weekly meetings ensured alignment

**Risk: Low Participation**
- Started promotion 2 weeks before the event
- Used social media, posters, and word-of-mouth
- Result: 67 registrations exceeded the 50-person target

**Risk: Technical Failures**
- Tested registration system, projector, and WiFi beforehand
- Kept backup equipment and mobile hotspot ready

**Risk: Budget Overrun**
- Maintained a detailed expense tracker
- Kept a 10% contingency buffer
- Final spend was 2.3% under budget

---

> *Risks were monitored throughout the project and addressed proactively.*
