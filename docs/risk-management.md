# ⚠️ Risk Management Plan

## Purpose

Identify potential risks that could impact the successful execution of the college tech event, and define mitigation strategies to minimize their impact.

---

## Risk Register

### 🔴 High Priority Risks

| # | Risk                            | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy                                                  |
|---|--------------------------------|-------------|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Low participant turnout        | Medium      | High   | Start promotion early; use multiple channels; offer incentives       |
| 2 | Venue unavailability           | Low         | High   | Book venue 3 weeks in advance; identify backup venue                 |
| 3 | Key team member unavailable    | Medium      | High   | Cross-train team members; document all processes                     |

### 🟡 Medium Priority Risks

| # | Risk                            | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy                                                  |
|---|--------------------------------|-------------|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 4 | Technical issues (WiFi, projector) | Medium   | Medium | Test all equipment 1 day before; arrange backups                     |
| 5 | Budget overrun                  | Low         | Medium | Track expenses weekly; maintain 10% contingency buffer               |
| 6 | Speaker cancellation            | Low         | Medium | Confirm speakers 1 week before; have backup speaker list             |

### 🟢 Low Priority Risks

| # | Risk                            | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy                                                  |
|---|--------------------------------|-------------|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 7 | Poor weather on event day       | Low         | Low    | Choose indoor venue; have indoor backup plan                         |
| 8 | Delayed promotional materials   | Low         | Low    | Set design deadlines 3 days before needed; use digital alternatives  |
| 9 | Registration system downtime    | Low         | Low    | Test system thoroughly; prepare manual registration backup           |

---

## 🛡 Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy    | When to Use                                | Example                                  |
|-------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Avoid**   | Risk can be eliminated entirely            | Book venue early to avoid unavailability  |
| **Mitigate**| Reduce probability or impact               | Cross-train members for backup coverage   |
| **Transfer**| Shift risk to another party                | Use venue's AV equipment instead of own   |
| **Accept**  | Risk is low impact and unlikely            | Accept minor weather disruptions          |

---

## 📋 Risk Monitoring Plan

- **Weekly Check-ins** — Review risk register during team meetings
- **Risk Owner Assignment** — Each risk assigned to a specific team member
- **Escalation Protocol** — High-impact risks escalated to Team Leader immediately
- **Post-Event Review** — Analyze which risks materialized and update for future events

---

## 🔄 Contingency Actions

| Scenario                        | Contingency Action                                     |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| < 20 registrations by Week 3   | Extend registration; increase promotion efforts        |
| Venue cancelled last minute     | Shift to backup venue or convert to virtual event      |
| Team member drops out           | Redistribute tasks; bring in volunteer support          |
| Internet failure at venue       | Use mobile hotspot; have offline presentation backups   |

---

> *Risk management is an ongoing process. This document is reviewed and updated at every team meeting.*
