# 📅 Timeline

## Project Schedule

| Week   | Phase                      | Key Activities                              |
|--------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Week 1 | Planning & Task Assignment | Define goals, assign roles, create strategy |
| Week 2 | Execution                  | Design, develop, promote, coordinate        |
| Week 3 | Review & Finalization      | Testing, dry run, final review, event day   |

---

### Week 1 — Planning & Task Assignment
- Formed the team and assigned roles
- Defined event objectives and theme
- Created project timeline and budget
- Set up communication channels

### Week 2 — Execution
- Poster and promotional materials designed
- Registration system built and deployed
- Venue booked and speakers confirmed
- Social media promotion launched

### Week 3 — Review & Finalization
- Tested all systems and materials
- Conducted dry run meeting
- Executed the event successfully
- Collected feedback and wrote final report

---

> *Timeline was reviewed and adjusted during weekly team meetings.*
